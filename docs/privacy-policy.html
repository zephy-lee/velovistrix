Privacy Policy

VeloVistrix — viewer for PhotoPrism
Effective 6 September 2026

VeloVistrix is a read-only gallery viewer for a PhotoPrism server that you host and control. It is an independent third-party client and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by the PhotoPrism project.

In short: this app collects nothing. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking SDKs of any kind. It talks only to the server address you type in.

Information we collect

None. The developer operates no servers and receives no data from this app. There is no account to create, no telemetry endpoint, and no third-party service embedded in the app.

Information stored on your device

Everything the app stores stays on your device:

  • Server address — the PhotoPrism URL you enter.
  • Session token — the credential your server issues at sign-in, kept in encrypted storage.
  • Cached photo metadata — album and photo listings, so the app can show your library without a network round trip.
  • Cached thumbnails and images — kept so browsing does not re-download the same picture repeatedly.
  • App settings — your display and slideshow preferences.

None of this is transmitted anywhere. You can erase all of it at any time by signing out or by clearing the cache in the app's settings; uninstalling the app removes it as well.

Network activity

The app connects to exactly one destination: the PhotoPrism server address you supply. It contacts no other host — no analytics endpoint, no content delivery network, no update server. Your photos travel between your device and your own server, and nowhere else.

Because your server is yours, whatever it logs is governed by your own configuration, not by this app.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — required to reach your PhotoPrism server.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used to tell "you are offline" apart from "your server is unreachable" when showing errors.

The app requests no access to your device's photos, camera, location, contacts, or storage.

Your photos are never modified

VeloVistrix is read-only by design. It never sends a write request to your server, so it cannot upload, edit, delete, archive, re-tag, or re-index anything in your library.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
