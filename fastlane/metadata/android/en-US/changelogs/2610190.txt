Save and share, plus six new ways to browse.

Save originals to your gallery, or share them to any app — one photo from the viewer, or several at once by long-pressing the grid. Progress shows while they download, and you can cancel.

The menu tab now opens people, calendar, regions, folders, labels and moments. Each list splits into Images and Videos tabs, the same as albums do.

In a photo's details, double-tap any value to copy it, and tap the location to open it in a map app.
